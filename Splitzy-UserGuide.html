Splitzy — User Guide

Bill splitter with tip · v1.3.5

What is Splitzy?

Splitzy splits one or more bills across a group of people, adds a tip, and tells you what each person pays. It works entirely on your device — there's no account, no server, and nothing you enter ever leaves your browser. It also installs as an app and works offline.

Splitzy is for splitting shared bills evenly and telling you the per-person amount. It does not track who-owes-whom over time (that's a job for a group expense settler).

Adding & editing bills

Add a bill: tap the + Add pill at the top-right of the Bills card. A small dialog opens — enter a description (e.g. “Dinner”), the amount, and a date (DD/MM/YYYY, defaults to today). Tap Add bill.

Edit a bill: tap the pencil icon on any bill. The row turns into editable fields right in place — change what you need and tap the tick to save, or the cross to cancel.

Delete a bill: tap the trash icon. A toast appears at the bottom with a 5-second Undo if you removed it by mistake.

The Subtotal below the list always shows the sum of every bill.

People & tip

In the Split Settings panel, set how many people are sharing and the tip percentage. You can either type a value directly into each field or use the − / + buttons. Tip defaults to 5% and steps by 1%.

Amounts can't be negative — Splitzy blocks negative entries automatically.

Round-Off

The Round-Off toggle sits next to the big result figure. Turn it on to round each person's share up to the next whole unit (handy for cash). When it's on, the amount collected will be slightly more than the exact total.

Currency

Use the currency selector at the top to choose ₹ INR (default), £ GBP, $ USD, € EUR, or No symbol if you'd rather stay currency-agnostic. Your choice is remembered next time.

Reading the result

The Result card shows the headline each person pays figure, then a breakdown: bills subtotal, tip amount, total with tip, and the per-person amount. It updates live as you change anything.

Saving a summary

Tap Summary and choose one of two options:

  • Image (on a computer) — downloads a PNG picture of the summary. On a phone or tablet this button is Share: it opens your device's share sheet so you can save the image to Photos/Files, or send it via message, email, WhatsApp, etc.
  • Text — copies a plain-text summary to your clipboard to paste anywhere.

Want a PDF on a computer? Use your browser's own print (Ctrl/Cmd + P) and choose “Save as PDF”.

Reset & Undo

The Reset button clears all bills and settings. Both Reset and deleting a bill show a 5-second Undo toast, so an accidental tap is easy to recover from.

Light & dark theme

Tap the sun/moon icon to switch between light and dark. Splitzy follows your device's preference by default and remembers a manual choice.

Installing & offline use

Splitzy is a Progressive Web App. In a supported browser you'll see an Install option (in the address bar on desktop, or “Add to Home Screen” on mobile). Once installed it opens like a normal app and works fully offline.

For the install option and offline mode to work, open Splitzy from a web address (https), not directly from a file.

Loading sample data (advanced)

For demos or testing, Splitzy can load a sample set of bills. There's no button for this — use any of:

  • Open the app with ?sample=1 added to the address.
  • Press Ctrl+Shift+L and pick a JSON file.
  • Drag a JSON file onto the window.

Loading is reversible via the Undo toast. You can regenerate the sample file with generate-data.ps1.
