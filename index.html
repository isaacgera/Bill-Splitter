About Splitzy

Split one or more bills with tip and rounding. Local-first & offline — nothing leaves your device.

Quick help

  • Add a bill — tap + Add (top-right of Bills), enter description, amount and date.
  • Edit / delete — use the pencil and trash icons on each bill. Deletes can be undone for 5 seconds.
  • People & Tip — type a value or use the −/+ buttons.
  • Round-Off — toggle beside the result to round each share up to the next whole unit.
  • Currency — pick from the selector; choose “No symbol” to stay currency-agnostic.
  • Summary — save the split as an Image, PDF or copy it as Text.
  • Reset — clears everything (undoable for 5 seconds).
